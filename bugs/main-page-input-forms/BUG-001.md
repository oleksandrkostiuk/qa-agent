# BUG-001: Newsletter form rejects valid email with generic error message

**Test Case:** TC-MAINPAGE-NEWSLETTER-001
**Feature:** main-page-input-forms
**Flow:** newsletter-signup
**Priority:** Critical
**Severity:** High
**Date Found:** 2026-07-23

## Description
The newsletter signup form in the footer rejects a syntactically valid email address ("qa-test-001@example.com") with a generic error message "There was a problem with your submission. Please review the fields below." This blocks users from subscribing to the newsletter with legitimate email addresses, preventing a key marketing feature from functioning.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Navigate to https://dev.skyline.glass/
2. Scroll to the footer newsletter signup form (Gravity Forms #5)
3. Enter "qa-test-001@example.com" into the email field
4. Click the Subscribe button

## Expected Result
Form submits successfully and a confirmation/success message is displayed to the user; no error is shown.

## Actual Result
Form re-renders with the error message "There was a problem with your submission. Please review the fields below." and the email field is marked as invalid (red border). The valid email remains in the field. The error persists when the form is re-submitted with the same email on a fresh page load (reproduced on retry).

## Console Errors
None recorded during execution. Network inspection notes: reCAPTCHA v3 iframe is present on the form, and the rejection may be related to bot-score detection, though this was not confirmed via network traffic analysis.

## Notes
- This issue was reproducible on two consecutive attempts from fresh page loads
- The email syntax is fully compliant with RFC 5322 standards
- Per test case precondition, the form is protected by invisible reCAPTCHA v3 and posts to a live Gravity Forms backend; automated submissions may receive a low bot score and be silently blocked, which may be the root cause
